## Description
Grab your phone and go for a walk. Every street you walk turns your color.
Connect your path into a loop, and everything inside becomes your territory.
TURF is a territory game played on the real streets of your city.

■ Walk to claim
• The streets you walk light up in your color
• Return to your start, or cross your own path, and the loop closes automatically
• See how much you own — measured in stadiums, parks and city blocks

■ Something new every day
• Treasure chests (wood, silver, gold) appear on the map daily — walk past to open them
• Enclose glowing gold tiles for bonus XP
• Make a near-perfect circle for the "Full Moon" bonus, chain loops for combos
• Morning Raid and Night Raid hours give 1.5× XP
• Weekend Fever: double XP from gold tiles, 1.5× from chests

■ From foot soldier to legend
• Level up and climb the ranks, all the way to "God of Turf"
• Daily missions, weekly goals, achievement badges and streaks
• Missed a day? A charm protects your streak
• Unlock new territory skins as you level up
• Switching phones? Keep playing with a transfer code

■ Compete
• Weekly, local, monthly, all-time and friends leaderboards
• The map shows exactly how far you are from the rival just above you
• Finish high in the monthly season to earn a season title
• Invite friends with a code and battle over tiles claimed this week (+200 XP for both of you)
• Share your territory as a card or a 6-second growth video

■ TURF Pro (one-time purchase, optional)
• 3 exclusive skins (Rainbow, Aurora, Night Sakura) that paint your territory tile by tile
• A gold frame for your share card
• Swap one daily mission per day
• Cosmetic and convenience only — rankings are never affected

■ Easy to keep going
• One gentle reminder at 7 pm, only on days you haven't walked (opt-in, scheduled on your device)
• A 20-second demo shows how to play without walking

■ Safe by design
• No sign-up. Names are generated automatically (no free text)
• Your path and precise location are never sent to our server
• No chat. Friends only see your name, level and tiles this week

Your walk keeps recording with the screen off in your pocket (when location is set to "Always").
Please don't look at your phone while walking. Stay off roads, railways, private property and restricted areas.
